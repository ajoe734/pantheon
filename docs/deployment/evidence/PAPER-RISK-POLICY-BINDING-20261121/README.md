## 1. Provenance-Backed Root Causes and Implemented Repairs

### 1.1 Profile Translation vs. Pool-Level Effective Limits (`RC2`)

As established by `PAPER-RISK-POLICY-PROVENANCE-20261121`:

1. **Requested Profile Translation:**
   - `POST /bff/personas` accepts `risk` (or `risk_level` / `risk_profile.risk_level`) and `risk_policy_ref` / `riskPolicyRef`.
   - `services/control-plane/bff/personas/service.py::_persona_create_canonical_payload` normalizes `riskPolicyRef` into `risk_policy_ref` even when an explicit `"risk_policy_ref": null` key is present in the payload, and normalizes `risk` from `risk_level` / `risk_profile.risk_level` when `risk` is omitted.
   - During artifact construction, the persona's requested risk profile translates to `StrategyArtifact.expression_policy.risk_profile` (`execution_style`) for signal expression.
2. **Where Effective Financial Limits and Policy Version Are Read:**
   - Pool-level financial limits (`gross_limit`, `net_limit`, `max_leverage`, `max_single_name_weight`, `max_sector_exposure`, `max_factor_exposure`, `turnover_limit`, strategy-family and correlation controls, `allowed_stages`, `allowed_asset_classes`, `forbidden_asset_classes`, `drawdown_actions`, `liquidity_constraints`) and `version` are owned by `services/capital/risk_policy.py` (re-exported at `services/control-plane/governance/risk_policy.py`).
   - At runtime, `services/execution/lean_runtime/expression_inputs.py::ExpressionInputsProvider` and `services/execution/lean_runtime/paper_runtime.py::PaperExecutionAlgorithm` call `services/capital/risk_policy.py::resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy` against the bound `CapitalPool` record (`/api/capital-pools/{pool_id}`).
   - `resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy` gives an explicit `CapitalPool.risk_policy_ref` precedence over `CapitalPool.metadata.risk_policy`, never reads risk policy limits from signal metadata, and preserves `require_approved_profile_mapping: bool | None = None` so that existing active persona paper pools with `rp-pool-persona-paper-*` inline v1 baselines and no approved profile mapping fail closed at `ExpressionInputsProvider` (`ExpressionInputsUnavailable("risk_policy", "... risk_policy_profile_mapping_unapproved")`) and `PaperExecutionAlgorithm._resolve_active_risk_policy()` (`RiskPolicyError("... risk_policy_profile_mapping_unapproved")`), while pools carrying a valid canonical `ApprovalEvidence` resolve the approved policy at runtime.
3. **Whole-Pool Fallback Removal, Default Required Profile Mapping, & Canonical Owner `ApprovalEvidence.require_valid` Verification:**
   - `resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy` and `resolve_pool_risk_policy_binding` reject pool-envelope mappings without policy content (`status="missing"`, `reason_code="risk_policy_missing"`) and reject empty `{}` or malformed inline `risk_policy` objects (`status="invalid"`, `reason_code="risk_policy_invalid"`).
   - On the normal path without any opt-in flag (`require_approved_profile_mapping: bool | None = None` in `resolve_pool_risk_policy_binding` and `resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy`, `require_approved_profile_mapping: bool = True` by default in `repair_paper_pool_risk_policy` and `PaperPoolRiskPolicyRepairRequest`, and ordinary `risk="low", risk_policy_ref=None` or minimal `_resolve_requested_pool_risk_policy` calls in `persona_provisioning_coordinator.py`), required owner profile mapping is enforced automatically on new and existing paper pools carrying a requested risk profile or an inline `rp-pool-*` / `rp-{pool_id}` baseline, and `requested_risk_profile`, `require_approved_profile_mapping=True`, and `profile_mapping_approval_id` are persisted on `pool["metadata"]` and carried through readback, runtime authority, pool-envelope publication/rebinding, fleet projection, and repair.
   - Attached metadata approval dictionaries (`profile_mapping_approval`, `approval_decision`, `approval_decisions`) are treated strictly as `decision_id` references and are never accepted as authoritative on their own; `_verify_policy_owner_approval_decision` never reads callable `approval_reader` objects from pool/binding metadata, requires canonical owner-store readback (`approval_reader.get(decision_id)`), wires a scoped read-only Capital owner approval transport (`CapitalOwnerApprovalReader` / `configured_capital_owner_approval_reader` calling `GET /api/capital-pools/{pool_id}/approvals/{decision_id}` and `GET /api/capital/approvals/{decision_id}` on `services/capital/main.py` using `PANTHEON_CAPITAL_API_URL` and `DEPLOYMENT_CAPITAL_SERVICE_TOKEN` / `RUNTIME_MANAGER_CAPITAL_SERVICE_TOKEN`) for `paper-signal-producer` and `paper-fleet-reconciler` / `paper_runtime.py` workers (plus pure-JSON `_ensure_default_pool_owner_transport` for the shared `pool-1`/`apv-pool-1` test fixture in `services/execution/lean_runtime/test_expression_inputs.py`), and validates every loaded owner record through `ApprovalEvidence.model_validate(...).require_valid(expected=..., now=..., usage_context=ApprovalUsageContext(...))` (enforcing `expires_at`, `controller_record_ref`, `recorded_at`, decider role authorization via `is_authorized_to_decide`, revocation/supersession, tenant/pool/version binding, `authorization_scope`, and `subject.risk_policy_digest` + `subject.requested_risk`).
   - All applicable lower bounds (`effective_from` across policy, policy metadata, pool metadata, binding, and binding metadata) and upper bounds (`expires_at` and `effective_to` across policy, policy metadata, pool metadata, binding, and binding metadata) are parsed and intersected (`max(effective_from) <= now < min(expires_at, effective_to)` and `max(effective_from) < min(expires_at, effective_to)`), failing closed on any expired secondary bound, future lower bound, empty intersection, or malformed timestamp.

### 1.2 Capital Guard, Full-Contract Digest Binding, and Risk-Reducing Availability

1. **Full Executable Dimension & Provenance Digest (`risk_policy_digest` & `pool_digest`):**
   - `services/capital/risk_policy.py::risk_policy_digest` hashes all executable dimensions in `POLICY_DIGEST_FIELDS` (including `max_sector_exposure`, `max_factor_exposure`, `turnover_limit`, `allowed_strategy_families`, `forbidden_strategy_families`, `max_strategy_family_concentration`, `max_target_overlap`, `max_signal_correlation`, `max_canary_capital_scale_pct`, `max_canary_gross_scale_pct`, order/TIF/liquidity/drawdown/pause/liquidation/kill-switch controls) plus approval-relevant provenance in `POLICY_PROVENANCE_DIGEST_KEYS` (`tenant_id`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `expires_at`, `superseded_by`, `stale`, `requested_profile`, `risk_profile`, `explicit_policy`).
   - `services/capital/main.py::pool_digest` hashes `risk_policy_digest(pool.metadata["risk_policy"])` whenever an inline `metadata.risk_policy` mapping is present on a `CapitalPool`, binding `CapitalPool.approval_digest` and repair convergence to the full effective contract.
2. **Fail-Closed Paper & Referenced Risk-Increasing Guard (`services/capital/capital_guard.py`):**
   - `CapitalGuard.authorize` validates both referenced (`risk_policy_ref` via `_require_risk_policy`) and inline (`metadata.risk_policy` via `_require_inline_paper_risk_policy`) policies through the common complete validator `resolve_pool_risk_policy_binding` and the canonical `RiskPolicyEvaluator().evaluate(policy, {**context, "target_type": target_type, "target_id": target_id, "capital_pool_id": ..., "risk_policy_ref": policy.risk_policy_id})`, enforcing expiry (`expires_at` / `effective_to`), effective window (`effective_from`), expected version/digest, tenant isolation, active status, stage, full multi-dimension evaluator limits and required observations (`allowed_asset_classes`, `forbidden_asset_classes`, `max_sector_exposure`, `max_factor_exposure`, `turnover_limit`, strategy-family and correlation controls, scalar/flex limits), and required profile mapping approval with zero test-fixture authorization exceptions in production code.
3. **Risk-Reducing Actions Remain Available (`allow_risk_reducing_actions=True`):**
   - Suspending or archiving a pool (`PATCH /api/capital-pools/{pool_id}/status` with `status="suspended"` or `"archived"`), suspending or revoking a binding (`PATCH /api/bindings/{binding_id}/status` with `status="suspended"` or `"revoked"`), applying a risk-decreasing rebalance, and freezing containment never call `CapitalGuard.authorize` and remain available even when a pool's inline or referenced risk policy is missing, invalid, expired, stale, or wrong-tenant.
   - At the runtime order risk gate (`PaperExecutionAlgorithm.EvaluateOrderRiskGate` -> `_resolve_active_risk_policy` -> `resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy`, as well as explicit `resolve_bound_pool_risk_policy(..., allow_risk_reducing_actions=True)`), when a bound paper pool has an unresolved or expired owner profile mapping (`status="unresolved_profile_mapping"` / `reason_code="risk_policy_profile_mapping_unapproved"` or expired policy/mapping timestamp) on an otherwise valid, active, same-tenant, paper-stage `RiskPolicy`, pure position-reducing and full-closing orders (`is_opening_or_increasing=False`, `is_sign_reversal=False`, `abs(q_after) < abs(q_before)`) resolve the bound `RiskPolicy` and evaluate under all applicable controls (active status, allowed stage, allowed/forbidden asset classes, exposure/leverage/single-name weight limits, `settlement_pending`, and price freshness), while opening orders, position-increasing orders, sign reversals, and non-reducing callers (`ExpressionInputsProvider`, direct `_resolve_active_risk_policy`) fail closed with `risk_policy_profile_mapping_unapproved`.

### 1.3 Lifecycle Coverage: New Pools, Ref-Only Creation, Existing Pools, Retries, Rebinding, Renewal, and Active `RuntimeBinding` Projection

1. **Provisioning & Governed Repair (`services/control-plane/bff/persona_provisioning_coordinator.py`):**
   - `_resolve_requested_pool_risk_policy` resolves ref-only creation (`risk_policy_ref` without inline `risk_policy`) through the policy owner (`CAPITAL_RISK_POLICY_DIR` / `load_risk_policy`) and preserves the owner's contract in `metadata.risk_policy` instead of writing a synthetic v1 `2.0` default snapshot.
   - `capital_pool_approval_digest` computes the canonical full pool digest matching `services/capital/main.py::pool_digest` (`pool_id`, `owner_id`, `owner_type`, `currency`, `budget`, `risk_policy_ref`, `single_runtime_enforced`, plus `risk_policy_digest(metadata.risk_policy)`). `_resolve_requested_pool_risk_policy`, `_coordinate_capital_pool`, `_publish_pool_envelope`, and `repair_paper_pool_risk_policy` (`owner_decision_res`, `target_validation`, and post-repair `after_resolution`) compute and verify the candidate full pool `approval_digest` consistently before any write and in readback, surfacing `candidate_pool_approval_digest` in `owner_decision_request` when unapproved.
   - `repair_paper_pool_risk_policy` (exposed at `POST /bff/personas/{persona_id}/provisioning/paper-capital/risk-policy`, alongside `GET /api/capital-pools/{pool_id}/risk-policy`) repairs genuinely missing policies on legacy paper pools and applies authoritative replacements (`risk_policy` or owner-resolved `risk_policy_ref`), but refuses to replace stale, expired, invalid, or foreign policy contents with the `2.0` default or clear stale markers when no authoritative replacement is supplied. Once converged, subsequent calls return `idempotent_noop=True, repaired=False` with zero `PATCH` writes.
2. **Historical Registry Admission Expiry vs. Ongoing Renewal Semantics:**
   - `ApprovalDecision` records with `target_type="registry_entry"` carry a 24-hour candidate admission `expires_at` window consumed when `advance_artifact_state` transitions the candidate `RegistryEntry` to `approved`. That historical admission timestamp does not revoke an already-approved running strategy artifact.
   - Ongoing paper pool trading, rebinding, and pool-envelope renewal are governed by active `CapitalPool` status, active non-stale `RiskPolicy` (`status="active"`, matching version/digest, effective window `effective_from`..`expires_at`/`effective_to`), and `PersonaCapitalBinding` effective window (`effective_from`/`effective_to`).
3. **Active Replacement `RuntimeBinding` (`runtimeBindingId`) Projection:**
   - `services/control-plane/bff/personas/service.py::_resolve_active_runtime_binding_overlay` and `services/control-plane/bff/personas/routes/detail.py::bff_get_persona` resolve active replacement `RuntimeBinding`s (via `replacement_parent_binding_id` / `replaces_binding_id`) so `GET /bff/personas/{persona_id}` (`runtimeBindingId`) projects the active v2 pool-envelope binding instead of the retired v1 binding.
4. **Deployment & Runtime-Manager Verification (`services/deployment/service.py`, `services/runtime-manager/deploy_authority.py`, `services/runtime-manager/fleet_desired_state.py`):**
   - `DeploymentService.create_plan`, `verify_deploy_authorities`, `PersonaProvisioningCoordinator.publish_pool_envelope`, and `build_fleet_desired_state` validate both inline (`metadata.risk_policy`) and ref-only (`risk_policy_ref` without inline policy) bound pool/plan/binding risk policies for existence, tenant match, active status, target stage, non-staleness, positive finite required limits, and preserve `profile_mapping_approval_id` / `approval_decision_id` / `approval_digest` references and canonical approval reader verification through fleet projection.
