## Delivered Scope

AG-DES-RS-001 landed the v4 Agora research facade schemas:

- `services/control-plane/specs/agora/v4/research_plan_execution.schema.json`
- `services/control-plane/specs/agora/v4/research_run_projection.schema.json`

Implementation commit `509e9d85` landed the schemas verbatim from the
authoritative `docs/04/pantheon_agora_cross_repo_2026-06-20/design-closure-round2/schemas/`
source and was merged via PR #2058 into `dev` at `ea5f8c7e`.

Coverage against the task brief acceptance criteria (B1–B10):

- **B1 plan-first**: `ResearchPlanExecution.status` lifecycle
  (`draft/approved/running/completed/cancelled`); runs may not bypass
  an approved `ResearchPlan`; `rejection = cancelled + terminal_reason =
  approval_rejected` encoded in `approval.state`.
- **B2 lifecycle**: Full stage status enum
  (`pending/ready/blocked/queued/running/completed/failed/cancelled/skipped`).
- **B3 typed stage→backend routing**: 12 canonical `stage_type` values; 11
  typed `preferred_backend` values; `fallback_policy` restricted to
  `fail_closed | explicit_fixture_only`; no silent stub fallback; LLM
  provides stage intent only, route policy resolves backend.
- **B4 ResearchRunProjection fields**: `lineage_refs`, `backend`
  (`requested/effective/mode`), `execution_status`, `outcome`,
  `metrics`, `findings`, `evidence_refs`, `data_cutoff`,
  `no_order_route_proof = "research_only_not_direct_action"`.
- **B5–B10**: Stage DAG with `dependencies` array; progress tracking
  (`phase/percent/completed_units/total_units`); `blocking_reasons`;
  `budget` tier control; `run_ids` association from plan to runs.

No fields, routes, or enums were invented beyond the design document.
Agora does not place orders, bind capital, or write `RuntimeBinding`
(governed handoff only builds a request).

Claude review approval is recorded in `ai-status.json` under
`review_notes_zh` (approved 2026-06-21). Frozen v1/v1.1/v1.2 schemas
and bundle indexes were not modified.
